Dataset & model management

Upload a labelled CSV with narrative and product columns. CivicCare will analyse it, evaluate the category classifier, and create unassigned Submitted tickets in the staff work queue.

Each valid CSV row becomes an unassigned Submitted ticket. Imported tickets are marked as dataset imports and appear in the staff work queue. Re-uploading the same file will skip matching rows.
